# 💫 About Me:

Building cross-platform AI applications with Flutter/Dart, focused on turning modern research into reliable, user-facing products.

My work spans both **on-device** intelligence (latency-first, privacy-aware) and **cloud-first** systems (scalable, tool-rich), with a strong interest in agentic frameworks and RAG-based architectures.

### Collaboration
- Looking for hackathon teammates who like moving fast without sacrificing engineering quality.
- Open to open-source contributors soon—especially people excited about agent workflows, retrieval pipelines, evaluation, and production AI UX.

### Ask me about
- Cars & automotive engineering
- Tech hardware and performance trade-offs
- Any sort of software (Operating systems, applications)
- Anything mechanical (systems, design, debugging, and why things fail)
Always eager to exchange knowledge—whether it's debugging an ESP32, discussing BERT implementations, or geeking out about car audio systems. Open to collaborations that push technical boundaries.
